- **Moderate** ✅：Assumed de novo, but without confirmation of paternity and maternity. Points based system for each unrelated proband determined by phenotypic specificity. Total of **1 point** will arrive at **Moderate**. 

Dravet\*: 1 points

Genetic Epilepsy with Febrile Seizures Plus: 0.5 points

Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathy: 0.5 points

Hemiplegic migraine: 0.25 points

Other epilepsy types or syndromes not included above, with or without associated neurodevelopmental features: 0.25 points
- **Supporting** ✅：Assumed de novo, but without confirmation of paternity and maternity. Points based system for each unrelated proband determined by phenotypic specificity. Total of **0.5 points** will arrive at **Supporting**. 

Dravet\*: 1 points

Genetic Epilepsy with Febrile Seizures Plus: 0.5 points

Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathy: 0.5 points

Hemiplegic migraine: 0.25 points

Other epilepsy types or syndromes not included above, with or without associated neurodevelopmental features: 0.25 points


#### PP1
- 默认强度：Pathogenic Supporting
- ACMG原始定义：Co-segregation with disease in multiple affected family members in a gene definitively known to cause the disease.
Note: May be used as stronger evidence with increasing segregation data.
- **Strong** ✅：Co-segregation with disease in multiple affected family members.

\>=7 independent meioses
- **Moderate** ✅：Co-segregation with disease in multiple affected family members.

5-6 independent meioses
- **Supporting** ✅：Co-segregation with disease in multiple affected family members.

3-4 independent meioses


#### PP3
- 默认强度：Pathogenic Supporting
- ACMG原始定义：Multiple lines of computational evidence support a deleterious effect on the gene or gene product (conservation, evolutionary, splicing impact, etc.).
