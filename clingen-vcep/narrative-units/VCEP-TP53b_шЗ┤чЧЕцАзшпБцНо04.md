#### PS2
- 默认强度：Pathogenic Strong
- ACMG原始定义：De novo (both maternity and paternity confirmed) in a patient with the disease and no family history.
Note: Confirmation of paternity only is insufficient. Egg donation, surrogate motherhood, errors in embryo transfer, etc. can contribute to non-maternity.
- **Very Strong** ✅：≥ 8 points
- **Strong** ✅：4-7 points
- **Moderate** ✅：2-3 points
- **Supporting** ✅：1 point


#### PS3
- 默认强度：Pathogenic Strong
- ACMG原始定义：Well-established in vitro or in vivo functional studies supportive of a damaging effect on the gene or gene product.
Note: Functional studies that have been validated and shown to be reproducible and robust in a clinical diagnostic laboratory setting are considered the most well-established.
- **Strong** ✅：Non-functional on Kato et al. data **AND** loss of function (LOF) by the majority of other eligible assays
- **Moderate** ✅：Partially functional on Kato et al. data **AND** loss of function (LOF) by the majority of other available assays


#### PS4
- 默认强度：Pathogenic Strong
- ACMG原始定义：The prevalence of the variant in affected individuals is significantly increased compared to the prevalence in controls.
Note 1: Relative risk (RR) or odds ratio (OR), as obtained from case-control studies, is >5.0 and the confidence interval around the estimate of RR or OR does not include 1.0. See manuscript for detailed guidance.
Note 2: In instances of very rare variants where case-control studies may not reach statistical significance, the prior observation of the variant in multiple unrelated patients with the same phenotype, and its absence in controls, may be used as moderate level of evidence.
- **Strong** ✅：≥ 4-7.5 points
- **Moderate** ✅：2-3.5 points
- **Supporting** ✅：1-1.5 points
