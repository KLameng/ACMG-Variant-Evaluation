#### BS4
- 默认强度：Benign Strong
- ACMG原始定义：Lack of segregation in affected members of a family.
Caveat: The presence of phenocopies for common phenotypes (i.e. cancer, epilepsy) can mimic lack of segregation among affected individuals. Also, families may have more than one pathogenic variant contributing to an autosomal dominant disorder, further confounding an apparent lack of segregation.
- **Strong** ✅：Lack of co-segregation with disease in pedigree(s) with a combined\* Bayes Likelihood Ratio<sup>f</sup> \<0.05​. 

\*For multiple pedigrees, results are combined. Recommended segregation analysis tool: COOL (COsegregation OnLine) v2 [http://fengbj-laboratory.org/cool2/manual.html](http://fengbj-laboratory.org/cool2/manual.html)
- **Supporting** ✅：Lack of co-segregation with disease in pedigree(s) with a combined\* Bayes Likelihood Ratio<sup>f</sup> >0.05 & ≤0.48.  

\*with multiple pedigrees, results are combined. Recommended segregation analysis tool: COOL (COsegregation OnLine) v2 [http://fengbj-laboratory.org/cool2/manual.html](http://fengbj-laboratory.org/cool2/manual.html)
